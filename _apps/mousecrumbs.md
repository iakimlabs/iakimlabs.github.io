**Mouse Crumbs** is a calm game about a curious little mouse and its small adventures on a cosy table.

Tap the bag of grain to scatter some treats — the mouse runs over and eats the crumbs one by one. Touch the mouse and it stops, squeaks and wriggles. When the crumbs run out, it goes back to its burrow, then comes out again, stands on its hind legs and sniffs around. Sometimes it says "pi", sometimes "pi-pi", sometimes nothing at all. Ring the bell to call it out right away, or turn on the automatic feeder, which adds new treats three seconds after the last crumb is eaten.

Made for relaxed play: no timers, no scores to chase, no ads. The game is suitable for children, and it never collects personal information.

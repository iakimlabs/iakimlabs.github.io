Process

How it works

  1. 1

    You prepare a closed test

    Upload a build to a Closed testing track in Play Console, add our testers' Google accounts (we send you the list) and share the web opt-in link with us.

  2. 2

    Testers opt in and install

    We enroll more than the required 12 testers, so a single drop-out doesn't restart the 14-day count.

  3. 3

    14 days of daily use

    Testers open your app on different phones and Android versions, stay opted in for the whole period and report bugs and impressions.

  4. 4

    Feedback report

    You get a summary of issues found, devices used and suggestions — useful both for your app and for the production access form.

  5. 5

    Apply for production

    We help you prepare answers about your testing and the changes you made. Google reviews the request and makes the final decision.

What you get

  • More than 12 testers opted in for the full 14 days
  • Daily app usage on a range of real Android devices
  • Bug reports and honest UX feedback during the test
  • A final report you can use in the production access form
  • Help with the store listing and a privacy policy page if you need one
  • Communication in English or Russian

What we need from you

  • A working build uploaded to a closed testing track
  • Our testers' emails added to the track's tester list
  • The web opt-in link (looks like play.google.com/apps/testing/…)
  • A short description of what to test and how
  • A contact for questions during the test

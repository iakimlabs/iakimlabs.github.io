Closed testing

Your first release, without the 12-tester headache

New personal Google Play accounts must run a closed test with at least 12 testers for 14 days in a row before they can publish. We provide engaged testers who actually open your app every day and tell you what they think.

See how it works
  1. 01

    Share your test link

    Set up a closed testing track and send us the opt-in link.

  2. 02

    Testers join

    We opt in more than 12 testers, so one drop-out won't reset your clock.

  3. 03

    14 days of real use

    Daily sessions on different devices, plus written feedback.

  4. 04

    Apply for production

    We help you answer Google's production access questions.

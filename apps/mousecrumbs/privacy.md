## Information handled by the app

Mouse Crumbs is an offline game. The app does not collect or transmit personal information to IakimLabs or third parties. It does not require an account, contain advertising, analytics or third-party tracking SDKs, or offer in-app purchases. It does not access your location, contacts, camera, microphone, photos or advertising identifier.

## Local storage and security

The app saves gameplay preferences on your device: portion size, movement speed, eating time, sound preference and automatic feeding preference. These settings are used only to remember your choices. Generated game sound files are stored in the app cache. This information is kept in Android app-private storage and is not sent to the developer by the app.

Settings remain until you clear the app storage or uninstall the app. Android backup or device-transfer services may handle app settings according to your device configuration and the service provider's privacy policy. IakimLabs does not receive or control those backups. You can manage them through your device or backup-provider settings.

## Children

The game is intended to be suitable for children, including children aged 5 and under and ages 6–8. The same no-collection practices apply to all players. There are no accounts, advertisements, chat, user-generated content or in-app purchases. We do not knowingly collect children's personal information through the game. Parents or guardians can contact us with privacy questions or concerns.

## Support correspondence

If you choose to email us, we receive your email address and the information you include. We use that information only to respond to your request and resolve the issue. Our email provider processes correspondence to deliver and store email. We do not sell correspondence or use it for advertising. We retain support messages only as long as needed to resolve the request and meet applicable legal obligations. You may request deletion by emailing us. Parents should contact us on behalf of children and avoid sending unnecessary personal information about a child.

## Voluntary closed testing

If you volunteer for a closed test and provide your Google-account email, we use it to add you to the tester list in Google Play Console and to communicate about the test. Google processes tester access through Google Play. We do not publish tester email addresses. We retain the list for the duration of the test and remove your entry on request or when testing ends. Feedback is used to improve the game; please avoid including personal information about children.

## External services

Google Play and Android may independently process information under their own privacy policies, for example for app distribution, device backups or platform diagnostics. This policy describes the Mouse Crumbs app and IakimLabs' handling of support correspondence. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages and may process technical request information as described in our [website privacy policy](/privacy/).

## Changes and contact
